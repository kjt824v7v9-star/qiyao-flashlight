About Us

A flashlight manufacturer and exporter in Huizhou Daya Bay

Huizhou Qiyao Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. is based in Daya Bay, Huizhou, Guangdong, manufacturing and exporting flashlights and related portable lighting for importers, wholesalers and private-label brands.

Drawing on the Pearl River Delta's electronics, hardware and battery supply chain, we take customers from model selection and sampling through to volume shipment, and prepare the documentation your destination market requires.

We work B2B. What you need is consistent quality, a repeatable lead time and pricing that survives the next order — not a one-off deal.
